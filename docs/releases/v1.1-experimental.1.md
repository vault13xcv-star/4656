# Brutal Morrowind 1.1 Experimental 1

Separate experimental version based on V1.0. The stable V1.0 release is unchanged.

- Original uploaded `burnt` and `poisoned` DDS artwork coats fire/poison corpses.
  Shared periodic stochastic maps hide seams and regular tile patterns while
  preserving the gradual transition, original clothing alpha and world lighting.
- `cut_limb` covers limb, neck and head cuts on physical disk/rim geometry.
- `cut_body_torso` covers waist cuts on elliptical cross-sections.
- `sliced_body` supplies separate left/right longitudinal anatomy. Posed mesh
  caps fit the atlas silhouette row by row, sharing one body-height frame and
  keeping the spine on the back.
- Wound surfaces receive ambient, sunlight, point lights, fog and native shadows.
  All five DDS files are checked against the uploaded originals during packaging.

Extract the entire Windows ZIP into a new directory and launch `openmw.exe`.
Your Morrowind data and existing OpenMW configuration are required. Game data,
the launcher and the editor are not included. Use copies of your saves for testing.

Combat, loot, physical wound rules and V1.0 blood performance controls are retained.
