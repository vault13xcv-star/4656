# Physical projectiles — experimental 1.2-projectiles.1

Based directly on stable Brutal Morrowind 1.1 (tag v1.1). The blood DDS, corpse materials, dismemberment rules and melee combat remain from that baseline.

Flight uses swept projectile contacts with full world gravity. Arrows/bolts align with velocity; thrown weapons rotate in simulation time. Physical actor contacts count as ranged hits without a second random miss roll. On impact the projectile model is retained without a world/inventory reference, so it cannot be picked up; the old random ammunition recovery is removed.

Actor impacts attach at the contacted surface to the closest pose bone. At the transition to a ragdoll, the attachment is rebound to a separate rigid body, including split torso halves. Dead actors are checked against visible scene geometry because native corpse actor collision may be disabled. Hard-surface contacts reflect a reduced normal velocity and transfer the remnant to the same item solver as books, bottles and other clutter. Item activity count, duration, pause, gravity, collision and budget settings apply. Collision shapes are bounded convex approximations; ricochet is dissipative, not a material penetration simulation. Water impacts retain ripples and remove the projectile.

Organic NPC/creature entry wounds follow the same attachment and emit at half the ordinary limb-wound rate, for 5 seconds times the status-duration setting (limb wounds: 10 seconds). The blood-amount setting still applies. Mechanisms, skeletons, ghosts and other excluded nonflesh families do not bleed.

Last-hostile slowdown is queued on a newly observed actor death and applied at the next whole simulation frame. The enemy test matches the rest restriction: actor-processing radius, live hostile combat/aggression, follower exclusion and water-only creature exclusion. Other nearby enemies prevent the effect. It multiplies the existing simulation speed by one third for two unpaused real seconds; it does not change the world's calendar time scale. Repeated deaths do not extend an active effect. Physics, animation callbacks, particles and scripting use the common simulation clock. All playing audio including music, voices and sounds flagged NoScaling is refreshed at the same transition; the previous speed is restored at expiry. Loaded corpses do not trigger it.

Impact remnants expire after 600 wall-clock seconds or leaving the player's current cell, whichever occurs first. They have a 1024-remnant safety cap and are not saved; loading a save clears session remnants. Changing exterior cells therefore also clears old remnants. No Morrowind game-data playtest or measured gameplay FPS is claimed. Verify modded skeleton attachments, ricochets, visibility and audio in an actual game. Use a separate installation and copied saves.
